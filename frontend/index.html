⚠ Sensor Context & Limitations ▶

ARGUS is designed to operate on thermal/low-light and optical footage simulating drone-camera imagery at border zones. The following challenges are inherent:

●Low-resolution thermal imagery — reduced spatial detail compared to RGB sensors.
●Thermal blooming — heat sources bleed into adjacent pixels, blurring object boundaries.
●Absence of RGB color/texture — limits fine-grained object discrimination.
●Partial occlusion — subjects may be obscured by terrain, vegetation, or other objects.
●Temporal ambiguity — rapid frame changes can cause identity-switching between tracks.
HONEST NOTE: Persistent tracking (BoT-SORT) and multi-frame trajectory analysis provide additional context across frames, but do NOT completely solve these challenges. Movement-pattern classification is a prototype heuristic, not a trained pose model. Human operator review is essential.
